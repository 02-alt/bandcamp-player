Cabin support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email TalkToBtbr@pm.me — we usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

My albums don't show up. Open Cabin, click + › Sync Bandcamp. A large collection can take a minute the first time.

Cabin asks me to sign in again. Your Bandcamp session expired. Open Cabin › Settings › Account and sign in again — your library and settings stay as they are.

Where are my downloads? In your Music folder, under Vinyl. Settings › Library › Show in Finder opens it.

Is Cabin made by Bandcamp? No. Cabin is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bandcamp.
