Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

Cabin does not collect, store or sell any personal data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Your Bandcamp account

When you sign in, your Bandcamp session is stored only in your Mac's Keychain and is sent only to Bandcamp, to load your collection and to stream and download the music you own.

Your library

Your library, playlists, play history and settings stay on your Mac. If you use Cabin's iCloud features, that data is stored in your own private iCloud account, which we cannot access.

Information lookups

To show lyrics, credits, artist bios, artist locations and concert dates, Cabin sends artist, album and track names — never your identity — to these public services: LRCLIB, Genius, MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, Discogs, Wikipedia/Wikidata, Last.fm, Bandsintown and the iTunes Search API. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

Shared recaps

When you share a yearly recap, its contents travel inside the link itself (after the #), which browsers never send to a server. The recap page renders it entirely in the viewer's browser; nothing is logged or stored.

Children

Cabin is not directed at children under 13.
